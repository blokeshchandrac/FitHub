FitHub Privacy Notice

FitHub is a university software-engineering demonstration that uses synthetic evaluator accounts and test data. This notice describes the demonstration data flows; it is not professional legal advice or a production privacy policy.

Information used

FitHub may store registration and profile information, membership status, attendance, workouts, progress, classes, notifications, and equipment or parking operational events associated with the demonstration.

Payments and simulations

Stripe Test Mode handles payment-card input, and FitHub does not store full card details. The smart-equipment and parking laboratories generate simulated operational events rather than physical-device observations.

Access, correction, and retention

Role-based access limits information according to Member, Trainer, and Administrator responsibilities. In a real deployment, users may request account correction or removal. Necessary security and audit records may be retained for system integrity.

Fitness guidance

FitHub does not provide medical diagnosis or professional nutrition advice. Guidance is generated from stored demonstration activity and profile information.
