FitHub Terms of Use

FitHub is a university software-engineering demonstration. It is not a commercial gym service. Synthetic evaluator accounts and test data are used to demonstrate the application.

Demonstration data

When you register, FitHub may store your account and profile information, memberships, attendance, workouts, and operational events needed to demonstrate its features. Equipment and parking laboratories are simulations.

Payments and fitness information

Payments use Stripe Test Mode. FitHub does not store full payment-card numbers or security codes. Training and nutrition information is general demonstration guidance and is not medical diagnosis or professional nutrition advice.

Account records

In a real deployment, a user may request correction or removal of their account information. Necessary security and audit records may be retained where required to protect system integrity.

This concise academic notice is not professional legal advice or a production service agreement.
